CR_CAM-IF is a tool to test the camera. It appears to be the same as FR_CAM-IF, but for a different model of 3DS. The 3DS will be made to take photos of color charts, where an operator will confirm that the camera has taken the photo correctly.

In order to run the software, extract the CR_CAM-IF folder and lineNumber_CR_CAM-IF.txt to the root of the C:\ drive. lineNumber_CR_CAM-IF.txt must contain a 4 digit number in plaintext and nothing more, otherwise CR_CAM-IF will fail to boot. You may need to open and close the "debug" screen (top left button) before you can control the software.
